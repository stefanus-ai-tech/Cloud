# Sorry Is a Beginning
Episode 09: an original goose story about apologizing and forgiving.

## Deliverables
Five separate portrait PNGs in numerical story order. Simple flat 2D cartoons with the corrected goose anatomy: curved neck, oval torso, folded side wings, two legs and webbed feet. The same grey songbird and lavender flower continue through the story. All pictures were generated individually with built-in imagegen; final prompts are in image-prompts.txt.
English narration: 144 words. Target: approximately 60 seconds. No audio or finished video has been generated, so the timing below is an initial plan. Reuse the established Philosophy Goose ElevenLabs v3 voice. The existing voice-design-prompt.txt is included.

## Voice and articulation
Paste narration-60s-elevenlabs.txt into ElevenLabs. This file contains only the spoken narration.
One narrator, the goose, tells the whole story. Read quoted dialogue with a subtle change of feeling rather than a second character voice. The friend uses she/her in the story; her appearance is an ordinary cartoon bird.
Warm, mellow, gently thoughtful delivery. Start around 150–155 spoken words per minute with short pauses. Keep the emotional weight small and believable, like telling a friend what happened. Allow the closing joke a little space.
These directions are a performance guide, not spoken text.

## Claude editing prompt
Create a 60-second English TikTok story from the five supplied PNGs and the recorded narration. Canvas 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Use the images in numerical order. Preserve the goose, bird, hat, flower and corrected bird anatomy throughout. Keep every scene as a separate shot. Use proportionate scaling, comfortable side margins, and preserve the feet and pot.

Start with the timing table, then align each cut to its narration paragraph. Use gentle 2–4% push-ins, small lateral drift, simple cuts or very short dissolves. Keep the flat cartoon style. Do not add detailed rendered backgrounds.
Place accurate English subtitles in the empty ivory upper area, at most two lines, dark charcoal with restrained sage or lavender emphasis. Match spoken words exactly. Keep captions clear of TikTok overlays. A small channel logo may be added if supplied.

Scene 1: the pot has broken and the flower droops; the friend is hurt.
Scene 2: the goose owns the mistake and asks how to help.
Scene 3: a new pot and watering show action after the apology.
Scene 4: the physical gap shows the goose respecting the friend's need for space.
Scene 5: the friend has chosen to forgive; a new flower bud suggests time and care. The final line about walking carefully gives the story a small smile.

The last image is a calm resting pose. Use a tiny restrained drift on the closing joke rather than inventing walking limbs. If animating anything, keep wings as feathered side wings, feet webbed and movement anatomically coherent.
Use soft unobtrusive instrumental music well below the voice. Hold a brief beat after “It didn't,” and before “Very, very carefully.” Do not turn the apology into melodrama. The friend's forgiveness is her choice; avoid captions implying forgiveness is owed or trust must return immediately.
If the audio ends early, hold the last frame to reach 60 seconds. If it is too long, shorten pauses or revise delivery without cutting spoken words. Export MP4 and an editable project if supported.
