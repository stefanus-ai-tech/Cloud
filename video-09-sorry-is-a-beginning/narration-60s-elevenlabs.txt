I knocked over my friend's flowerpot.
The pot broke.
She went quiet.
I said, 'Sorry!'
and waited for everything
to feel normal again.
It didn't.

I wanted to say,
'I didn't mean it.'
But that wouldn't mend the pot.
So I said,
'I broke something you cared about.
What can I do to help?'

She asked me to replace the pot.
I did.
We replanted the flower,
and I came back to water it.
An apology needs somewhere to go
after the words.

She still needed space.
So I gave her some.
Forgiveness wasn't something
I could demand.
And forgiving me wouldn't mean
pretending nothing happened,
or trusting me immediately.

Later, she said,
'I forgive you.'
We sat beside the flower.
It had a new bud.
Some things can grow again,
with care and time.
I still walk around her pots.
Very, very carefully.
