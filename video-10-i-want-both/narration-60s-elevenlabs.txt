I wanted to leave the pond
and see the world.
I also wanted to stay
where everything felt safe.
Apparently, I wanted freedom
with a familiar doorstep.

I wanted my friend's company.
Then I wanted an afternoon alone.
Was I being dishonest?
Maybe I wanted connection
and enough room
to hear my own thoughts.

I wanted to build something impressive.
I also wanted a quiet day.
Ambition asked for effort.
Rest asked me to stop.
Both had a voice.
Neither could run the whole day.

Some desires pull against each other.
Some share a root:
wanting a life that feels like ours.
I didn't need to shame either wish.
I needed to choose
what mattered now.

So I took a short adventure,
then came home.
Made time for my friend,
and for myself.
I couldn't have everything
at once.
Especially swimming
and staying completely dry.
Still working on that one.
