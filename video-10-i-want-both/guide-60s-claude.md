## Claude editing prompt
Create a 60-second English TikTok using the five PNG files in numerical order and the recorded narration. Output 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps. Keep each picture as a separate shot. Preserve the corrected goose anatomy and flat cartoon style. Scale proportionately and keep the hat, feet and props inside comfortable margins.

Use the timing table as an initial guide, then place transitions at the actual narration paragraph boundaries. Add subtle 2–4% push-ins or gentle drift, simple cuts or very short dissolves. Use readable English subtitles in the blank ivory upper area, maximum two lines, dark charcoal text with restrained sage or lavender emphasis. Match the spoken words exactly, and keep text away from TikTok overlays.

Scene 1: an open gate, outward path and familiar pond show adventure and security pulling in different directions.
Scene 2: the grey friend and the book in its separate resting space suggest connection and solitude.
Scene 3: the stone stack and cushion suggest effort and rest.
Scene 4: two branches with a shared root illustrate that some different desires can come from a shared wish. This is a philosophical metaphor, not a scientific diagram.
Scene 5: hold the hesitant foot above the water for the swimming-without-getting-wet joke. The narration mentions several choices before the punchline; use the actual images rather than inventing new scenes.

Keep background music quiet and unobtrusive. Give the last joke a beat. Avoid added captions promising that all conflicting desires can be satisfied or that everyone has the same underlying motive.
If the narration finishes early, hold the last frame to reach 60 seconds. If it exceeds the target, shorten pause spacing or adjust the voice take before cutting words. Export MP4 and an editable project if supported.

## Story framing
This is an original philosophical goose fable. It explores wishes that overlap, wishes that compete for time and attention, and choices that involve tradeoffs. The shared-root line is the goose's reflection about some desires, not a claim about every person. The story includes both making room for different wants over time and recognizing an impossible combination in the closing joke.
